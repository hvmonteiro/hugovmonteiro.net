Early-stage teams have to learn quickly while working with limited time and resources. I enjoy helping founders think through the technology and delivery choices behind an idea, drawing on my experience in systems architecture, integration, software delivery, and cross-functional work.

## Conversations I can help with

- **The problem and the first product.** Identify the user need, the assumptions to test, and a sensible first scope.
- **Technology choices.** Discuss build-versus-buy, architecture, integration, security, and the trade-offs of moving fast.
- **Delivery and team habits.** Shape a way of working that makes progress visible and keeps decisions reversible where possible.
- **Growing with intention.** Consider when a system, process, or team needs more structure—and when it does not.

Mentoring is a conversation, not a ready-made playbook. I ask questions, share relevant experience, and help founders make their own decisions with greater clarity.

If you're building something and think my perspective could help, introduce your idea and the decision you're facing on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hvmonteiro/).
